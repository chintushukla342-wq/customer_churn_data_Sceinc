# Project Summary

## Objective
Demonstrate an end-to-end workflow for predicting customer churn in a subscription business.

## Data
1,200 synthetic customer records. The data is created for learning and portfolio demonstration only.

## Analysis performed
- Checked row counts, duplicate records, unique customer IDs and missing values.
- Explored churn distribution and churn rates by contract, autopay and internet service.
- Compared tenure, monthly charges and support calls across churn groups.

## Modelling
Built preprocessing pipelines and compared Logistic Regression and Random Forest using a stratified train/test split. Evaluated accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score and ROC-AUC.

## Limitations
Synthetic data cannot establish real-world business performance. Validate any findings on representative, permissioned company data before operational use.
